Understanding Total Compensation

An offer is never just base salary.

Total compensation = base + signing bonus + annual bonus % + equity value + benefits value. A $100K base with $20K signing bonus and 15% annual bonus is different from $100K base alone.

Cost-of-living matters: $150K in San Francisco (COL ~220) has less purchasing power than $100K in Austin (COL ~120).

Negotiation Scripts: Three Common Pushbacks

These scripts are starting points. Adapt to your situation.

Them: "This is our standard offer for your level."
You: "I'm excited about the role. Based on my research, comparable roles range $X to $Y. My background puts me at the higher end. Can we move on base salary or signing bonus?"
Them: "We can't go higher; equity budget is locked."
You: "I understand. Since equity's constrained, could we revisit base salary or increase the signing bonus?"
Them: "We're already fair to everyone."
You: "I believe you are. Negotiating is professional norm, not ingratitude. Based on my research, there's room to move."
