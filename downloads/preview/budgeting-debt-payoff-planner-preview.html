What is zero-based budgeting?
Every dollar you earn is allocated somewhere: savings, debt, expenses, or goals. It's not about restriction—it's about control. You decide where your money goes instead of wondering where it went.
The three categories:
Fixed expenses: Rent, utilities, insurance, minimum debt payments. Variable expenses: Groceries, gas, personal. Goals: Savings, extra debt payments.
The rule:
Income = Fixed + Variable + Goals. If they don't match, cut variable expenses or adjust goals.

02Snowball vs. Avalanche — Comparison

Two mathematically different strategies. One saves money; one saves sanity.

Snowball method
Pay off smallest balance first, then next smallest. Psychological effect—quick wins build momentum. Example: $5K CC, $8K loan, $20K student debt. Pay the CC first for the psychological win.
Avalanche method
Pay off highest interest rate first, then next highest. Mathematically optimal—saves the most money on interest. Same example: Target the 24% CC first because that interest rate is killing you fastest.
