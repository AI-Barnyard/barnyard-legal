Student Study Planner & Habit Tracker

A semester-length planner for students juggling multiple classes: weekly study-load calculator, spaced-repetition review tracker, and daily habit grid.

Not a substitute for professional help. If you're struggling with time management, mental health, or learning disabilities, talk to your school's student support services. Study tools work best alongside proper sleep, exercise, and breaks.

01Weekly Study Load Calculator

Enter each class with estimated weekly study hours and difficulty. See your total load and get warned if you're overcommitted.

For each class: estimate weekly study hours (lectures + assignments + reading). Difficulty is just a weight for your own sense of drain: 1 = easy/review, 2 = normal, 3 = hard.
