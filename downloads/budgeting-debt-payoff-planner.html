03Irregular Income Note

For freelancers and gig workers: Use your average monthly income (total annual income ÷ 12) in the budget worksheet above. In months where you earn more, allocate the extra to your ”extra payment” in the debt calculator or to emergency savings. This creates a buffer for lower-income months.

The snowball method often works better for irregular income because you build momentum with quick wins (paid-off debts), while the avalanche method requires more discipline to stick with when income varies.
