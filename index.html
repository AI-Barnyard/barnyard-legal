01Who we are

Barnyard Templates ("Barnyard," "we," "us") makes and sells digital templates and planning tools, distributed through our own storefront, our Instagram account @barnyardtemplates, and Pinterest. This policy covers the data we collect through those channels and through the storefront that processes template purchases.

02What we collect

We collect only what a given interaction actually requires:

  • Contact details you give us directly — an email address at checkout, or whatever you include in a message to us.
  • Order information — which templates you bought, when, and the license associated with the purchase.
  • Basic device and usage data collected automatically by the platforms we operate on (browser type, approximate location from IP, pages viewed), the same way most storefronts and social platforms do.

We do not ask for information we don't need to fulfill an order or respond to a message — no birthdate, no phone number, no physical address, unless a specific product genuinely requires shipping.

03Purchases & payment

Checkout is handled by Stripe, a third-party payment processor. We never see or store your full card number — Stripe handles that directly and shares back only what we need to fulfill the order (amount, last four digits, billing email). Stripe's own privacy policy governs how it handles payment data.

04Instagram & Pinterest

When you follow, message, comment, or save from us on Instagram or Pinterest, that activity is governed by that platform's own privacy policy and terms — we only see what the platform's normal creator tools show any account owner (follower counts, public comments, saves, and message content sent to us directly). We use that information to run the account and respond to you, not to build outside profiles of who you are.

05Who we share it with

We share data only with the services that make Barnyard run, and only the minimum each one needs:

  • Stripe — to process payment and deliver receipts.
  • Instagram and Pinterest — to publish content and receive your follows, comments, and messages on those platforms.

We do not sell contact or order information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing purposes.

If that ever changes — a new tool genuinely needs to touch customer data to keep Barnyard running — this page gets updated first, not after.

06How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as needed for receipts, license support, and basic bookkeeping. Messages are kept as long as useful for context on an ongoing conversation. You can ask us to delete anything we hold on you that isn't a tax or accounting record we're required to keep — see Contact.

07Your choices

You can unfollow or block us on Instagram or Pinterest at any time, using those platforms' own controls. You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by writing to the address below.

08Children

Barnyard's products and accounts are meant for a general adult audience. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13, and we'll delete it if we learn we have.

09Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in any way that matters, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. Continuing to use Barnyard's storefront or accounts after a change means you've seen it.

10Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request about your data, go to clawopener@gmail.com or a direct message to @barnyardtemplates.
